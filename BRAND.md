# Brand: PATHWAYS

## 0. Brand Stance

PATHWAYS is an operational project-information system for humanitarian and development organizations.

The brand should communicate:

1. **Institutional trust** — sensitive project and Beneficiary information is handled deliberately.
2. **Operational clarity** — users should understand scope, status, evidence, and next actions quickly.
3. **Human judgment** — monitoring and rule-based support inform people; they do not impersonate autonomous decision-makers.

PATHWAYS should feel calm, reliable, accountable, and useful under everyday program-monitoring work.

## 0.1 Surface Modes

### Staff Workspace
- deep navy navigation
- white workspace
- blue actions
- compact role/scope context
- bordered operational surfaces
- restrained elevation
- semantic status colors

### Access / Authentication
- light-blue/white gradient
- centered identity card
- PATHWAYS mark/name
- concise instructions

### Public Transparency
- approved external information only
- white/subtle body surfaces
- navy hero
- larger public headings
- aggregate/non-sensitive project information
- explicit approved-public framing

## 3. Color Character

PATHWAYS uses a restrained institutional palette:

- Navy `#0B2E4F` — structural shell/public hero
- Blue `#0072CE` — primary actions/selection
- White `#FFFFFF` — primary workspace
- Subtle neutral `#F7F9FB` — low-emphasis panels
- Green `#1F7A4D` — success/on-track
- Amber `#8A4B08` — warning/at-risk
- Red `#B42318` — destructive/critical
- Slate `#172B3A` / `#526779` — primary/secondary text

Semantic status colors must always be paired with readable text.

## 5. Imagery and Evidence

PATHWAYS is evidence-first.

Prefer:
- charts
- approved project media
- safe maps
- the existing PATHWAYS mark
- evidence/media with explicit access and publication provenance

Avoid:
- decorative stock humanitarian imagery
- fake Beneficiary portraits
- fabricated project photos
- imagery that implies verified impact when it is only a placeholder

Private evidence remains private until separately approved for publication.

## 6. Voice

PATHWAYS copy is:
- direct
- calm
- factual
- privacy-aware
- explicit about human review
- honest about unavailable/incomplete states

Prefer:

> Monitoring rules are unavailable in the current API.

over vague or playful failure copy.

Prefer:

> Approved public project information

when public content is specifically approval-controlled.

Avoid:
- AI-powered/autonomous wording
- guaranteed-impact claims
- user-facing `prototype`, `mock`, `demo`, `presentation-only`

Valid workflow labels such as **Quick Preview**, **Staff Preview**, and **Public Preview** remain acceptable when they describe real preview behavior.

## 7. Anti-References

Do not move PATHWAYS toward:
- generic purple SaaS gradients
- glass-heavy staff dashboards
- gamification
- tiny terminal-style tables
- color-only status systems
- gratuitous animation
- fabricated data used to keep screens full
- AI-chat styling for deterministic rules
